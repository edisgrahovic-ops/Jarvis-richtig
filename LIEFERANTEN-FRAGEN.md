# Fragen an den Lieferanten (zum Weiterleiten)

Diese Fragen klären **Marge**, **Versand/Zoll-Ablauf** und die **rechtlichen
Pflichtangaben** (GPSR-Herstellerdaten, Textil-Material, Pflegehinweise,
Warnhinweise, Chargennummer, Bildrechte). Kopiere den englischen Block unten
komplett und schick ihn an den Lieferanten.

> Wichtig danach: Die Antworten zu **Material, Herstellername + Adresse + E-Mail,
> Chargennummer, Warnhinweisen, Pflegehinweisen** füllen die roten Marker im Shop.
> Die **Einzelpreise** und **DDP/IOSS** brauchen wir für Marge und Lieferzeit.

---

## English message to the supplier

Hello,

we are launching an online store in Germany (EU) and would like to work with you on a **dropshipping** basis. Before we start selling, we need to clarify a few things.

**These questions are very important for us, and it would help us a lot if you could answer each of them precisely and individually.** Correct answers on shipping, customs and product details are essential for us to be able to sell legally in the EU.

**1. Pricing (per single order)**
- Price for **1 single pullover** shipped individually to a customer in Germany (product + shipping)?
- Price for **1 single neck pillow** shipped individually to Germany?
- Any price discounts at higher volumes (e.g. 50 / 100 / 500 units per month)?
- Any additional fees per order (handling, dropshipping fee)?

**2. Shipping & fulfillment**
- Do you ship **directly to our customers** (dropshipping)?
- Is the shipment **blind / white-label** — no supplier invoice, no supplier logo, no pricing inside the parcel?
- Shipping cost **per single order** to Germany?
- Which carrier do you use, and do you provide a **tracking number** for every order?

**3. Delivery time**
- Processing/handling time before shipping?
- Delivery time to Germany after shipping?
- Total delivery time a customer should expect?

**4. Customs, import VAT & duties — VERY IMPORTANT**
- Do you ship on a **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** basis, so all customs duties and import VAT are **prepaid** and the customer pays **nothing extra** on delivery?
- Do you use an **IOSS number** to handle EU import VAT for orders under €150, so parcels clear customs without charging our customer?
- Can you **confirm in writing** that our customers will **NEVER** be asked to pay additional customs duties, import VAT, or carrier handling/clearance fees on delivery?
- Which **country do the parcels ship from** — China, or do you have an **EU / German warehouse**?
- If you have an **EU/German warehouse**: price and delivery time from there?
- What **customs value** will be declared on the parcel? Please declare it **correctly and legally** (no under-declared values and no fake "gift" parcels).
- What **HS / customs tariff code** do you use for these products?
- What **customs paperwork / commercial invoice** is attached, and does it stay **blind** (no supplier name and no wholesale price visible to the customer)?
- Who is listed as the **importer of record**?
- Do you offer both **standard and express** shipping? Cost and delivery time for each?
- Is the tracking number **trackable with the German last-mile carrier** (e.g. DHL / Deutsche Post)?
- How do you handle parcels that are **lost, stuck in customs, or heavily delayed** — refund or free reshipment?

**5. Product & legal/compliance data (required by EU law)**
- Exact **material composition** in % (e.g. "100% polyester")?
- Exact **dimensions and weight** for each item?
- **Care / washing instructions** and washing symbols?
- For EU product safety (GPSR): the **manufacturer's legal company name, full address and email**?
- A **model number / batch number** we should display?
- Any **safety warnings** or warning symbols?
- Any **certificates or test reports** (e.g. CE, OEKO-TEX, quality/test certificate)?

**6. Branding / customization**
- Can you add **our logo / a custom label / custom packaging**?
- If yes, the **minimum order quantity (MOQ)** and the extra cost?

**7. Stock & reliability**
- Are both products **currently in stock** for continuous dropshipping?
- How do you handle **out-of-stock** situations, and what is the **restock lead time**?

**8. Returns, defects & lost parcels**
- What happens if a product arrives **defective, wrong, or is lost** — refund or free replacement?
- Who pays for **returns**, and what is the **return address**?

**9. Product images & usage rights**
- May we use your **product photos and videos** on our website, social media and paid ads?
- Can you confirm this **usage permission in writing**?

**10. Ordering & payment**
- How do we **place dropshipping orders** (manually, CSV, API, app)?
- Which **payment methods** do you accept, and are there minimum order requirements?

We would really appreciate detailed and precise answers to every point above, as this information is critical for us before launch.

**Please also excuse us if any question has been asked twice** — we simply want to make sure nothing important is missed.

Thank you very much, and we look forward to working with you.

Best regards,
Stowe Studio
