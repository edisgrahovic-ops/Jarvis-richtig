# Fragen an den Lieferanten (zum Weiterleiten)

Diese Fragen klären **Marge**, **Ablauf** und die **rechtlichen Pflichtangaben**
(GPSR-Herstellerdaten, Textil-Material, Pflegehinweise, Warnhinweise,
Chargennummer, Bildrechte). Kopiere den englischen Block unten und schick ihn
an den Lieferanten.

> Wichtig danach: Die Antworten zu **Material, Herstellername + Adresse + E-Mail,
> Chargennummer, Warnhinweisen, Pflegehinweisen** füllen die roten Marker im Shop.
> Die **Preise pro Einzelbestellung** brauchen wir für die echte Marge.

---

## English message to the supplier

Hello,

we are launching an online store in Germany (EU) and would like to work with you
on a dropshipping basis. Before we start selling, we need some information.
Could you please answer the following questions?

**1. Pricing (per single order)**
- What is the price for **1 single pullover** shipped individually to a customer in Germany (product + shipping)?
- What is the price for **1 single neck pillow** shipped individually to Germany?
- Are there price discounts at higher volumes (e.g. 50, 100, 500 units/month)?
- Are there any additional fees per order (handling, dropshipping fee)?

**2. Shipping & fulfillment**
- Do you ship **directly to our customers** (dropshipping)?
- Is the shipment **blind / white-label** — i.e. **no supplier invoice, no supplier logo, no pricing** inside the parcel?
- What is the shipping cost **per single order** to Germany?
- Which shipping carrier do you use, and do you provide a **tracking number** for every order?
- Are customs duties and import taxes **fully included** in the price (no extra charges for the customer on delivery)?

**3. Delivery time**
- How many days for **processing/handling** before shipping?
- How many days for **delivery to Germany** after shipping?
- What is the **total delivery time** a customer should expect?

**3b. Customs, import VAT & duties — VERY IMPORTANT**
- Do you ship on a **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** basis, meaning all customs duties and import VAT are **prepaid** and the customer pays **nothing extra** on delivery?
- Do you use an **IOSS number** to handle EU import VAT for orders under €150, so parcels clear customs without charging our customer?
- Can you **confirm in writing** that our customers will **NEVER** be asked to pay additional customs duties, import VAT, or carrier handling/clearance fees on delivery?
- Which **country do the parcels ship from** — China, or do you have an **EU / German warehouse**?
- If you have an **EU/German warehouse**: what are the price and delivery time from there? (Faster, and no customs per order.)
- What **customs value** will be declared on the parcel? Please declare it **correctly and legally** — we do not want under-declared or falsely marked "gift" parcels (this is illegal and risks our business).
- What **HS / customs tariff code** do you use for these products?
- What **customs paperwork / commercial invoice** is attached to the parcel, and does it stay **blind** (no supplier name and no wholesale price visible to the customer)?
- Who is listed as the **importer of record**?
- Do you offer both **standard and express** shipping? Please give **cost and delivery time for each**.
- Is the tracking number **trackable with the German last-mile carrier** (e.g. DHL/Deutsche Post)?
- How do you handle parcels that are **lost, stuck in customs, or heavily delayed** — refund or free reshipment?

**4. Product & legal/compliance data (required by EU law)**
- What is the exact **material composition** in %? (e.g. "100% polyester")
- What are the exact **product dimensions and weight** for each item?
- Please provide the **care/washing instructions** and washing symbols.
- For EU product safety (GPSR), please provide the **manufacturer's legal company name, full address and email**.
- Is there a **model number / batch number** we should display?
- Are there any **safety warnings or warning symbols** for the products?
- Do you have any **certificates or test reports** (e.g. CE, OEKO-TEX, quality/test certificate)?

**5. Branding / customization**
- Can you add **our logo / a custom label / custom packaging**?
- If yes, what is the **minimum order quantity (MOQ)** and the extra cost?

**6. Stock & reliability**
- Are both products **currently in stock** and available for continuous dropshipping?
- How do you handle **out-of-stock** situations, and what is the **restock lead time**?

**7. Returns, defects & lost parcels**
- What happens if a product arrives **defective, wrong, or is lost** in transit — do you offer a **refund or free replacement**?
- Who pays for **returns**, and what is the **return address**?

**8. Product images & usage rights**
- May we use your **product photos and videos** on our website, social media and paid ads?
- Can you confirm this **usage permission in writing**?

**9. Ordering & payment**
- How do we **place dropshipping orders** (manually, CSV, API, app)?
- Which **payment methods** do you accept, and are there minimum order requirements?

Thank you very much — we look forward to working with you.

Best regards,
Stowe Studio
